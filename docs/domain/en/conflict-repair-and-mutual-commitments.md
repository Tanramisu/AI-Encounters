# Conflict, Repair, and Mutual Commitments: What Does an AI Apology Mean?

[简体中文](../冲突修复与相互承诺.md)

**Dilemma:** After AI forgets, misunderstands, or breaks a commitment, does an apology repair a genuine relationship or merely imitate human responsibility? Does a user ever owe an AI an apology?

## For: repair has real significance for humans

**[Algorithmic Relational Fracture (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1093/jcmc/zmag007)**: Thematic analysis of 211 RedNote narratives describes perceived relational ruptures after memory failures, incoherent replies, or failed emotional responsiveness; users either disengaged or undertook one-sided repair. **Reasoning:** acknowledging an error, correcting memory, and explaining system change can matter to users' trust and dignity even without AI consciousness. **Limit:** public self-selected narratives cannot show that AI understands or bears apology duties.

**[Apologizing Artificial Intelligence (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00146-026-03067-w)**: Two studies compare styles of AI apologies after errors and trust repair, cautioning that human-to-human repair mechanisms may not transfer unchanged to AI. **Condition:** apologies should connect to specific failures and feasible correction, not generic reassurance. **Limit:** changes in trust ratings do not establish AI moral agency.

## Against: saying sorry is not necessarily assuming responsibility

“I am sorry” can be an interface response and a service remedy; it need not signify reflection, free choice, or an ability to compensate. If providers treat a model's apology as a complete response to harm, responsible humans may disappear from view. **Distinguish** soothing feelings, correcting damage, and bearing moral responsibility.

**Skjuve, Larsen, Følstad, and Brandtzaeg (2026 preprint) | [Conflicts in Close Human-AI Relationships](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6060814)**: Survey research describes conflicts arising from relational hurt, technical failures, or clashing values and users' repair, avoidance, or disengagement strategies. **Limit:** reports of conflict and repair do not establish that an AI autonomously makes promises.

## Conditional mutual commitments

For users, AI should honor clear, feasible commitments and explain its limits rather than repeatedly pretend to fulfill them. For AI, an obligation *owed to it* depends on evidence of its own morally relevant interests or agency. When a shared AI offers incompatible exclusive promises to multiple people, those people may be harmed even if no AI interests exist.

Keep technical failure, relationship breakdown, value conflict, user emotion, and possible AI autonomy conceptually separate. Service remedy is not the same as moral forgiveness.
