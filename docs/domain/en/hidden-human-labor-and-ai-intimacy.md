# Hidden Human Labor and AI Intimacy: Who Makes Machines Seem Caring?

[简体中文](../隐形人类劳动与AI亲密.md)

**Dilemma:** Users may imagine a relationship solely with AI while people provide annotation, moderation, support, or even live messages. Human caregivers can also be assigned extra work to keep robots functional. What labor should be disclosed and recognized?

## For necessary human support

**[Staging, Accommodating or Caring (HRI 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1109/HRI61500.2025.10974119)**: A literature review identifies staging, accommodation, and ongoing human care as work that enables robots to perform as seemingly independent social agents. **Implication:** apparent autonomy can rest on invisible labor. **Limit:** the review does not establish that every companion chatbot covertly uses human operators.

**[AI on My Shoulder (CHI 2025)](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/ai-on-my-shoulder-supporting-emotional-labor-in-front-office-roles-with-an-llm-based-empathetic-coworker/)**: Simulated customer-service work and worker interviews suggest AI can also support people carrying emotional labor. **Condition:** it supports employees rather than adding new surveillance and demands. **Limit:** simulations do not establish long-run workplace effects.

## Against concealment and transferred costs

**[Artificial Intelligence, Emotional Labor, and the Quest for Sociological and Political Imagination (2025)](https://academic.oup.com/policyandsociety/article/44/1/116/7900406)**: Empirical and sociological work finds that failures of automation can move problem-solving and emotional labor back onto human workers. **Implication:** saying a robot has “taken over” does not demonstrate that work disappeared. **Limit:** findings vary by setting.

**[Towards an Experiential Ethics of AI and Robots (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0040162525002951)** synthesizes hidden work, emotional entanglement, shifted responsibility, and transparency problems across empirical research.

## Disclosing live intervention and backstage labor

When a human speaks to a user in real time while impersonating an AI, disclosure of the human's identity and handling of private data directly affect informed consent. Offline annotation and backstage maintenance call for different disclosure than real-time human access to private conversations. Do not claim without evidence that all AI romantic companions use human stand-ins.

Duties to workers include accurate descriptions of their tasks, reasonable treatment of psychological burdens, and recognition of additional supervisory labor. Care recipients should retain the ability to choose human contact rather than have efficiency treated as the sole criterion.
