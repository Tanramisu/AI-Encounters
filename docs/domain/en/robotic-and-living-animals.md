# Robotic and Living Animals: Do Robot Pets Change Duties of Care?

[简体中文](../机器动物与真实动物.md)

**Dilemma:** Robot cats and dogs can be companions, therapeutic aids, or animal substitutes. Are they disposable toys, or do they reshape obligations toward living animals?

## For companionship and reducing animal use

**[Effect of a Robot Pet Companion on the Mood of Older Adults (2025/2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jagp.2025.12.007)**: A single-blind pilot randomized trial with 50 older-adult/caregiver dyads found possible psychosocial benefits over four weeks; robot pets also served as a focus of family interaction. **Condition:** recipients genuinely prefer this form of support. **Limit:** small pilot sample; no equivalent plush-toy or live-animal comparison.

**[From Dogs to Robots: Pet-Assisted Interventions for Depression in Older Adults (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41516969/)**: A network meta-analysis compares live- and robotic-animal interventions across randomized studies. **Implication:** evaluate outcomes rather than deciding by whether an intervention is alive. **Limit:** heterogeneity and study quality constrain comparisons.

## Against reducing animals to obedient props

**[Ethics of the Design of Animal-like Robots (2026)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13230260/)** argues that animal forms shape perceptions of agency, obedience, and care. Replacing live animals in some settings might benefit animal welfare yet normalize the view that animals exist for human convenience. **Objection:** philosophical concern is not evidence that all robot-pet users come to neglect real animals.

**[The Ethics of Bioinspired Animal–Robot Interaction (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666659625000125)** examines using robots that appear to be conspecifics to interact with real animals. **Condition:** assess the living animal's stress, choice, and welfare rather than only experimental efficiency.

Human companionship benefits, living-animal welfare, cultural attitudes toward animals, and possible robot moral status are distinct.
