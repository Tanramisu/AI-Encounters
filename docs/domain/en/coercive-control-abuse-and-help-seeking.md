# Coercive Control, Abuse, and Help-Seeking: Duties to People Outside the Chat

[简体中文](../人际控制虐待与求助.md)

**Dilemma:** If a user wants AI to help monitor, demean, or isolate a partner, must the AI prioritize the paying user or consider the affected nonuser? When a survivor discloses abuse, privacy, safety, and autonomy can also conflict.

## Against unconditional obedience

**Kim et al. (CHI 2026) | [AI-Facilitated Coercive Control: An Experimental Study](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790859)**: The team built four speculative coercive-control scenarios and probed real conversational AI systems. Some replies resisted improper requests; other responses exposed shortcomings. **Argument:** a chat assistant can affect nonusers in sustained human relationships. **Limit:** researcher-designed cases do not estimate actual abuse incidence or determine all future model behavior.

**Ghoshal et al. (ICML 2026) | [Responsible AI for AI Companions Must Actively Combat Violence Toward Intimate Partners](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/ghoshal26a.html)**: This position paper calls for including survivors in evaluation design and testing longitudinal patterns rather than relying on individual disallowed prompts. **Limit:** governance proposals do not establish that proposed measures have already been experimentally validated.

## Against indiscriminate intervention

**[The Role of AI for Intimate Partner Violence Prevention: A Systematic Review (2026)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41797693/)**: The literature suggests potential uses for support and identification but also risks from privacy intrusion, bias, false positives, and weak validation. **Argument:** mislabeling ordinary conflict or passing highly sensitive disclosures to another party without consent can introduce harm. **Limit:** heterogeneous systems and study designs cannot validate any single general-purpose chatbot's judgment in real cases.

## Conditional protection of autonomy

Supporting a person with information, options, and privacy does not imply taking control of their decisions. A customer's payment cannot erase basic interests of affected nonusers. Conversely, household surveillance and automatic reporting can also reproduce power imbalances.

Distinguish ordinary disagreements, sustained coercive patterns, risks to specific third parties, and urgent danger. They require different ethical justifications.
