# Public Space, Bystanders, and Nonusers: May People Refuse an AI They Never Chose?

[简体中文](../公共空间旁观者与非用户.md)

**Dilemma:** Public robots, smart-home cameras, meeting assistants, and wearable AI record and analyze people who never selected the service. How can bystanders refuse or seek remedies?

## For useful environmental assistance

Navigation for disabled people, directions for visitors, and summarizing meetings can benefit users. **Condition:** the user's convenience is not consent by everyone nearby to recording, identification, retention, or third-party sharing. Brief sensory processing, persistent storage, and disclosure have different stakes.

## Against ownership as universal consent

**[Designing Privacy Choice in Generative AI Chatbot Ecosystems (CHI 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790809)** investigates privacy choices across personalization and data sharing and users' needs for upfront and contextual control. **Implication:** nonusers' interests need consideration when a robot surrounds them. **Limit:** chatbot privacy design is not an empirical prevalence study of public-robot harms.

**[Families' Vision of Generative AI Agents for Household Safety (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757598)**: Co-design with 13 parent–child dyads raises memory segregation, conversational consent, and selective sharing. **Condition:** cohabitation does not make every person's information freely available to whoever purchased the device. **Limit:** preferences do not establish legal rules or successful technical controls.

## Bystander awareness and data interests

**Mihailov and Wangmo (2025) | [Does Humanness Matter?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11948-025-00547-y)** discuss how bodily assistance and human contact in care may involve different dignity and privacy interests. **Implication:** serving one person with a robot also requires attention to the boundaries of others present.

Separate active users, bystanders, and particularly vulnerable people. Ask whether ongoing tracking is required, whether processing can remain ephemeral, and whether affected people understand the observation. Declining a conversation, declining recording, and demanding the absence of all sensors are different claims.
