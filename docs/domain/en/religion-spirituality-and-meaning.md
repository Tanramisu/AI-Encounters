# Religion, Spirituality, and Meaning: May AI Offer Faith Guidance?

[简体中文](../宗教精神与人生意义.md)

**Dilemma:** When AI interprets scripture, role-plays a sacred figure, joins prayer, or answers questions of life's meaning, is it a study aid or an unaccountable claimant to religious authority?

## For tool-assisted exploration

AI can retrieve texts, compare translations, organize interpretations, and facilitate personal reflection. Its value depends on checkable materials and users retaining interpretive agency, not on claiming revelation. A [2026 NPR/Ipsos survey](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/most-americans-skeptical-turning-ai-spiritual-guidance) found some American AI users already seek spiritual guidance, study scripture, or generate prayers. **Limit:** observed use does not establish correctness or authorization by religious communities.

## Against synthetic ultimate authority

**[We Are Building Gods (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11023-024-09667-z)**: A philosophical critique argues that treating a model trained on accumulated human speech as a superior authority risks surrendering ethical, spiritual, and democratic judgment to a synthesis of the past. **Condition:** the user actually defers uncritically. **Objection:** traditions also rely on inherited texts; accountable assistance is not automatically such surrender.

**[Sacred or Synthetic? Evaluating LLM Reliability and Abstention for Religious Questions (AIES 2025)](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIES/article/view/36543)** tests doctrinally differentiated Islamic jurisprudence questions for correctness and abstention and finds notable degradation in Arabic. **Implication:** confidently merging distinct religious interpretations may mislead believers. **Limit:** specific tasks and traditions do not represent every faith or ritual.

**Pew Research Center (2025) | [Americans' Views on AI](https://www.pewresearch.org/science/2025/09/17/how-americans-view-ai-and-its-impact-on-people-and-society/)**: A survey of 5,023 US adults found roughly 73% opposed AI involvement in advising on personal faith. **Limit:** opinions are neither a universal moral mandate nor a causal outcome study.

## Conditional respect

Scripture lookup, historical role-play, religious counseling, and presiding over ritual are different roles. An AI should not present one interpretation as the only legitimate teaching or portray “God told you” as an undisclosed factual revelation. Authority to hear confession, validate rituals, or provide pastoral leadership belongs to the relevant people and traditions, not automatically to software.
