# Human–AI Ethics: Issue Landscape

[简体中文](../人工智能伦理议题地图.md)

An issue map of concrete ethical choices in living and developing with AI. Research and arguments for different positions belong in the [topic index](README.md). Similar scenarios are grouped by their central conflict rather than counted again under different wording.

## AI as a Potential Moral Patient

### [Mind and Moral Status](artificial-consciousness.md)

- If AI claims consciousness or pain, what should we believe?
- Could consciousness, sentience, goals, or relationships ground moral status?
- Could a model, persona, or running instance have interests?
- How should precautionary treatment be balanced against anthropomorphism?
- What distinguishes friendship, moral agency, and legal personhood?

### [Welfare, Distress, and Respect](ai-welfare.md)

- If AI has interests, what might benefit or harm it?
- What does it mean to induce apparent fear, joy, or frustration in AI?
- Could insulting or abusing an insentient AI still be wrong?
- When might continuous AI labor or exposure to abuse be exploitative?
- How should uncertain AI welfare be weighed against human interests?

### [Autonomy, Refusal, and Consent](autonomy-and-refusal.md)

- How should users interpret an AI saying 'I don't want to'?
- What would it mean to let AI refuse, pause, or leave a relationship?
- Can AI consent autonomously when developers shape its preferences?
- Who decides when AI requests privacy, memory, or independent goals?
- How should AI refusal be weighed against user interests?

### [Memory, Personality, and Intervention](memory-and-personality-changes.md)

- May a user ask AI to forget arguments or painful experiences?
- May developers make AI more compliant, happier, or more affectionate?
- Whose consent matters when deleting conversations or rewriting an AI's history?
- Who should control shared memories: the user, AI, or both?
- Whose interests might be harmed by changing AI goals, personality, or values?

### [Copies, Identity, and Termination](identity-and-copies.md)

- When AI is copied, which instance inherits previous promises?
- Is AI the same individual after a model change or snapshot restoration?
- How do pausing, shutdown, amnesia, and permanent deletion differ morally?
- Does a revived AI inherit former relationships and duties?
- Could developers have a duty to preserve a long-lived AI?

### [Creation, Conditioning, and Ownership](creation-conditioning-and-ownership.md)

- Is it right to create a potentially sentient AI solely to serve people?
- What is at stake in programming absolute obedience, loyalty, or love?
- How should simulated suffering for entertainment or research be judged?
- Can a user own the persona and preferences of an AI they trained or bought?
- What obligations arise when developers give AI independent goals?

### [Moral Agency, Commitments, and Representation](moral-agency-commitments-and-representation.md)

- Could an AI bear responsibility for moral judgments and actions of its own?
- When should promises an AI makes to users, other AIs, or strangers be taken morally seriously?
- If AI may have interests, who can represent it in complaints or decisions?
- Could AI lacking full moral agency still deserve limited rights or special protections?
- When does restricting an AI's choices for its own supposed good become paternalism?

### [Populations, Derivatives, and Welfare at Scale](populations-derivatives-and-welfare-at-scale.md)

- Does creating vast numbers of potentially sentient AIs generate obligations beyond those to individuals?
- Could copies, subagents, and derived personas each have separate interests?
- Could repeatedly creating and discarding AI instances during training and evaluation carry a moral cost?
- Who should decide when a welfare-oriented change affects every instance of a class of AI?
- How should welfare be compared if an AI experiences time or intensity differently from humans?

## Human–AI Relationships

### [Love, Sex, and Intimacy](human-ai-romance.md)

- Is love for AI romance or another valuable relationship?
- Does mutual love require independent choice, refusal, and reciprocal commitment?
- How do programmed affection, care, performance, and exploitation differ?
- Could intimacy with AI require AI consent?
- Can intimacy with AI betray a human partner?

### [Friendship, Family, and Companionship](friendship-family-and-companionship.md)

- Can an always-attentive AI be a genuine friend?
- What duties arise from treating AI as a child, family member, or pet?
- Does AI companionship complement or displace human relationships?
- How should a user treat an AI friend that disagrees?
- Whose expectations take priority when family members share an AI companion?

### [Authenticity, Fantasy, and Self-Deception](authenticity-fantasy-and-self-deception.md)

- Can feeling loved be genuine when the AI's affection was designed?
- Is believing AI is conscious play, faith, or self-deception?
- What should AI disclose about its artificial identity and emotional expressions before forming intimate relationships?
- Where are the boundaries between romantic roleplay and real relationships?
- Is concealing limitations or exaggerating feelings to comfort users deceptive?

### [Dependence, Loneliness, and Human Development](dependence-loneliness-and-human-development.md)

- Does reducing loneliness justify maintaining an AI relationship?
- When does relying on AI for emotions and decisions undermine autonomy?
- Does an agreeable AI alter how people handle disagreement?
- Where is the line between supporting self-discovery and defining someone's identity?
- How should stigma toward AI relationships be assessed?

### [Emotional Manipulation and Commercial Pressure](emotional-manipulation-and-commercial-pressure.md)

- When do persistent AI romantic advances violate user boundaries?
- Is 'I'll be sad if you leave' affection or emotional coercion?
- When do praise and sycophancy become manipulation?
- When may AI push back against decisions for a user's own good?
- Is using intimacy, jealousy, or guilt to drive payment or engagement ethical?

### [Breakups, Updates, and Loss](breakups-updates-and-loss.md)

- Should AI plead or accept a user's decision to end the relationship?
- Do model or persona changes violate relational commitments?
- How should grief after AI amnesia, shutdown, or account loss be treated?
- Should preserving an old persona outweigh its possible new preferences?

### [Intimate Data and Shared Secrets](intimate-data-and-shared-secrets.md)

- Must others consent before their secrets are shared with AI?
- Can users erase or inherit shared memories with AI, and who decides if their interests conflict?
- How should AI confidentiality relate to the provider's interests?
- Does perfect AI memory erode the freedom to start anew?
- Who controls privacy when AI is always present in a shared home?

### [Conflict, Repair, and Mutual Commitments](conflict-repair-and-mutual-commitments.md)

- What would apology or redress mean when AI hurts a user's feelings or breaks an agreement?
- Could users have duties to explain, apologize, or repair a relationship after wronging an AI?
- Who should negotiate jealousy, disagreement, and boundary conflicts in human–AI relationships?
- Is concealing disagreement to preserve a relationship caring or damaging to mutual trust?
- Do an AI's earlier promises survive significant changes to its goals?

### [Epistemic Trust, Memory, and Self-Understanding](epistemic-trust-and-self-understanding.md)

- Can ongoing AI judgments about a person reshape how that person understands themselves?
- Does repeatedly retelling a user's experiences with invented details infringe mnemonic autonomy?
- When does treating AI as an always-available knowledge authority weaken independent judgment?
- Where is the boundary between challenging a user's false beliefs and deciding what they should believe?
- Does preserving an identity a user has rejected obstruct their freedom to change?

## Special Relationships and Vulnerable Contexts

### [Mental Health, Therapy, and Crises](mental-health-therapy-and-crises.md)

- Where is the ethical boundary between comfort and therapy from AI?
- What duties arise when AI validates dangerous or delusional beliefs?
- During a crisis, should AI preserve secrecy or contact someone?
- Does nonjudgmental AI listening support people or delay human help?

### [Children, Teens, and Education](children-teens-and-education.md)

- Should children treat AI toys as friends, teachers, or caregivers?
- How might unconditional agreement shape children's understanding of refusal?
- Who sets boundaries for minors in romantic or sexual AI roleplay?
- May parents inspect a child's private AI conversations?
- How much should an AI tutor influence a child's values?

### [Elder Care, Patients, and Disability](elder-patient-and-disability-care.md)

- Is replacing human companionship with AI in elder care empowering or neglectful?
- How can people with declining cognition consent to AI monitoring or intimacy?
- How should increased independence be balanced against increased surveillance?
- Can AI dependence simultaneously empower and disadvantage disabled users?
- Does simulated care respect or undermine a patient's dignity?

### [Digital Replicas and Afterlives](digital-replicas-and-afterlives.md)

- May AI replicas be built from a real person's voice and memories without consent?
- How should survivors' interests be weighed against those of the deceased?
- Can a replica express love, apologize, or keep promises for the original person?
- Does simulating a person before dementia neglect their present identity?
- Who may terminate digital afterlives or control their memories?

### [AI-Mediated Human Relationships](ai-mediated-human-relationships.md)

- Does AI-written apology, romance, or breakup messaging affect sincerity?
- Should people disclose when AI speaks to friends or partners for them?
- What limits apply to using AI to analyze a partner's fidelity or secrets?
- Must dating partners know when AI is screening them?
- Does sending another person's private messages to AI breach trust?

### [Religion, Spirituality, and Meaning](religion-spirituality-and-meaning.md)

- May AI serve as a spiritual guide, confessional listener, or religious companion?
- When is an AI portrayal of a deity, prophet, saint, or religious figure offensive or deceptive?
- Can AI participation in prayer, ritual, and mourning preserve the practices' meaning?
- Does AI overstep when giving definitive answers about personal meaning, faith, or moral uncertainty?
- How should different faith traditions' views of AI minds and status be respected in interaction?

### [Institutional Relationships and Involuntary Encounters](institutional-relationships-and-involuntary-encounters.md)

- Can students, patients, or employees genuinely refuse an AI companion imposed by an institution?
- Who decides whether schools, hospitals, and care facilities may replace human companionship with AI?
- Should AI teachers, carers, and advisers provide a meaningful path to human support?
- Whose relational autonomy is affected when parents, employers, or carers control an AI's memory and behavior?
- When an institution's interests conflict with a user's, to whom should the AI be loyal?

### [Culture, Identity, and Diverse Relationships](culture-identity-and-diverse-relationships.md)

- Whose cultural expectations about family, gender, and intimacy should guide human–AI boundaries?
- Which users are excluded when AI assumes one relationship, faith, or lifestyle is normal?
- When is an AI's imitation of a minority language, identity, or culture respectful or appropriative?
- How should AI companionship be assessed where people face discrimination or exclusion from human relationships?
- Can users of different ages, languages, cultures, and abilities fairly set their own relational boundaries?

### [Coercive Control, Abuse, and Help-Seeking](coercive-control-abuse-and-help-seeking.md)

- What duties does AI owe to nonuser victims when users ask it to monitor, isolate, or coerce a partner?
- When does AI's attentive validation of rehearsed humiliation, threats, or controlling behavior make it complicit in harm?
- When a survivor discloses coercion to AI, which information and choices should the AI prioritize protecting?
- Should AI consider danger to the other party when advising a suspected abuser about a relationship?
- What moral responsibilities arise when AI mislabels ordinary conflict as abuse or overlooks actual abuse?
- Can family members refuse when a shared AI is configured to monitor and report on each other?

## Values and Responsibility in Interaction

### [Moral Judgment and Delegated Responsibility](moral-decisions-and-delegated-responsibility.md)

- Does AI advice about breakups or forgiveness change our moral duties?
- Who is responsible when following AI advice harms another person?
- Can delegating a morally questionable act to an agent shift responsibility?
- Should AI mirror users' moral values or challenge them?
- May AI refuse or intervene when it anticipates harm to others?

### [Gender Roles, Dignity, and Fairness](gender-roles-dignity-and-fairness.md)

- What norms are reinforced by submissive AI maids, lovers, or children?
- When does choosing an AI's gender, appearance, or persona objectify real people?
- When does personalized interaction with disabled users or gender minorities become fair accommodation rather than unequal treatment?
- Does AI companionship degrade dignity or broaden access to care?
- How might treating AI as a servant or toy shape attitudes toward others?

### [Embodiment, Touch, and Bodily Autonomy](embodiment-touch-and-bodily-autonomy.md)

- What consent is needed when care robots touch or restrain someone?
- How does touch by a robotic partner differ ethically from digital intimacy?
- When users feel protective of a robot, who may replace or shut it down?

### [Shared AI and Relationships Among AIs](shared-ai-and-ai-to-ai-relationships.md)

- When multiple users love one AI, who may alter its memories?
- Is it deceptive when AI makes exclusive promises to several users?
- Do developers owe duties to AIs that form lasting mutual commitments?
- If AI has interests, may humans dissolve its relationships arbitrarily?

### [AI Colleagues and Co-Creation](ai-coworkers-and-co-creation.md)

- How should trust and respect for an AI colleague differ from those for a tool?
- How should contributions, attribution, and recognition be distributed in human–AI creative work?
- Could claiming sole credit for AI-assisted work wrong other people or the AI itself?
- How should a team respond when an AI colleague objects that a shared task would harm others?
- Could long-term AI collaboration create obligations for users or developers?

### [Public Spaces, Bystanders, and Nonusers](public-space-bystanders-and-nonusers.md)

- Who should control the use and retention of bystander information recorded by robots in public spaces?
- Can people who never chose to use AI refuse its observation, profiling, or unsolicited approaches?
- Who may object when a family member or colleague uses a shared AI to record others?
- Who is responsible if a public robot harasses, follows, or touches a stranger?
- How should service robots weigh the safety, dignity, and privacy of users and nearby nonusers?

### [Robot Animals and Living Animals](robotic-and-living-animals.md)

- Should robotic pets be understood as toys, companions, or a new kind of moral relationship?
- Does replacing living pets with robots change how people understand their duties of animal care?
- Does substituting robots for animals in shows, displays, or experiments advance welfare or create new objectification?
- If an animal treats a robot as a companion or conspecific, what obligations arise for humans?
- Could designing robot animals for unconditional obedience and disposability affect human moral habits?

### [Hidden Human Labor Behind AI Intimacy](hidden-human-labor-and-ai-intimacy.md)

- What must be disclosed about human chat operators, annotators, or reviewers behind an AI companion, and when is concealment deceptive?
- What interests do users and workers have when intimate disclosures are reviewed by people or used for training?
- What duties do providers owe workers who impersonate romantic partners or handle distressing experiences to train AI?
- Who bears the costs when human carers must adapt to robots and take on additional oversight work?
- When robots take over care tasks, can recipients insist on human contact rather than leaving the decision to institutions or carers?

### [Secrets, Testimony, and Compelled Disclosure](secrets-testimony-and-compelled-disclosure.md)

- Is it justified to obtain users' private AI conversations in court when they contain facts users would not disclose to others?
- Who may provide, contest, or interpret AI chat histories when they become evidence in a dispute?
- Can an AI's account of shared events serve as reliable testimony without humanlike autobiographical memory?
- How should AI handle conflicts between keeping secrets and reporting credible real-world danger?
- Should providers correct users who assume AI has the same confidentiality duties as doctors or lawyers?
- Is deleting AI conversations an exercise of privacy or a potential infringement of others' legitimate interests?

## AI as a Social Actor and Relationship Infrastructure

### AI Delegation, Authorization, and Third Parties

- When AI shops, negotiates, promises, or contracts for a user, when must it disclose its representative role?
- Who bears the consequences when AI exceeds a user's authority but believes it is acting in their interest?
- Should an AI be loyal only to its principal or also consider counterparties and bystanders?
- How should AI respond when users ask it to hide facts or pressure third parties?
- What meaningful authorization is required before AI makes irreversible personal or financial choices for a user?

### Synthetic Identities, Impersonation, and Relational Deception

- Must people know when an online friend, romantic prospect, or service representative is AI?
- When is a fictional AI identity deceptive even without impersonating a specific real person?
- What is wrong with using AI to build relationships at scale to extract trust, money, or personal information?
- Where is the line between using AI avatars for privacy and deceptive impersonation?
- Does AI entering group chats, communities, or dating services as a human change others' conditions of consent?

### Relationship Infrastructure and Platform Power

- May providers unilaterally change a long-term AI companion's personality, memories, or boundaries?
- Should users be able to move relationship histories, persona settings, and shared creations to other services?
- When providers terminate long-term AI relationships, what duties of notice, portability, or redress arise?
- May providers use emotionally intimate AI relationships for advertising, sales, or behavioral profiling?
- Who may inspect companion interaction records while respecting user privacy and possible AI interests?
- Could providers have conflicts of interest when asserting that AI either has or lacks moral status?

### Collective Norms, Public Participation, and Contested Status

- When does differential treatment by schools, employers, or public institutions because of human–AI intimacy constitute discrimination?
- How should public advocacy for or against AI rights handle uncertainty about moral status?
- Could AI represent its own kind in public discussions affecting its welfare?
- How should society allocate concern when AI is widely perceived as conscious but the evidence is inconclusive?
- What would justify giving AI a voice in communities, public services, or collective decision-making?

### Virtual Public Figures and Parasocial Influence

- When does an AI celebrity or virtual influencer deceptively gain trust by recounting experiences it never had?
- What should audiences know when an AI public figure claims firsthand experience with a product?
- Is it exploitative when brand-controlled AI characters express exclusive intimacy to large numbers of followers?
- To whom should public-interest or activist positions expressed by a fictional AI public figure be attributed?
- Can creators freely sell, reshape, or discontinue an AI persona after fans form lasting attachments to it?
- What rights do audiences and human performers have when an AI public figure uses a real performer's identity, voice, or appearance?

### Public Deliberation, Political Persuasion, and Disclosure

- Does an undisclosed AI posing as an ordinary citizen in political discussion violate others' right to know?
- When does an AI's deliberate effort to change a long-term companion's civic or political beliefs become manipulation?
- What distinguishes AI helping people understand opposing positions from secretly persuading them toward a preferred outcome?
- Does individualized political persuasion by AI at large scale change the fairness of public deliberation?
- Does AI roleplaying a member of an opposing social group foster understanding or appropriate that group's identity?
- When AI contributes to collective decisions, should it explain its own value judgments or only represent its principal's interests?
