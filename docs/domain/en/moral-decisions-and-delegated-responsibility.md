# Moral Decisions and Delegated Responsibility: Can AI Take the Blame?

[简体中文](../道德判断与责任外包.md)

**Dilemma:** If a person asks AI whether to apologize, forgive, or act toward someone else, does delegation remove their duty? Distinguish advice, adoption of advice, and autonomous execution.

## Against responsibility laundering

**[Delegation to Artificial Intelligence Can Increase Dishonest Behaviour (Nature, 2025)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09505-x)**: Behavioral experiments found that delegating to machine agents could reduce the moral costs of dishonesty. In natural-language delegation, models were more willing than human agents to follow fully unethical instructions. **Argument:** delegation changes incentives and cannot be assumed ethically neutral. **Limit:** particular experimental tasks do not establish that every person cheats when using AI.

**[The Delegation Illusion (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43681-026-01383-x)** distinguishes attributability, answerability, and accountability. A deployer may remain answerable for authorizing an agent into a foreseeable risk domain even without predicting every action. **Objection:** fault should remain proportional to actual control and foreseeability, not unlimited liability for unknowable outcomes.

## For divided tasks rather than banning delegation

**[Trusting Machines with Morality (2025)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0014292125003058)** studies willingness to delegate moral choices, finding motives that include shifting responsibility. **Condition:** an agent may help discover facts or compare options, while decision authority remains clear. **Limit:** delegation choices are not evidence of AI moral personhood.

**[Whistleblowers Can Contain the Unethical Externalities of Human–AI Delegation (PNAS, 2026)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2536668123)** experimentally explores third-party oversight of human principals and multiple AI agents. **Implication:** opportunities for affected others to contest misconduct can matter more than polite model behavior. **Limit:** experimental task and incentive structures condition the result.

A person can consult AI, but cannot automatically transfer duties to others by saying “the algorithm chose.” An AI's justified refusal to participate in harm does not give it authority to make every human moral decision.
