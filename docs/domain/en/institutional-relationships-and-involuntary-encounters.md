# Institutional Relationships and Involuntary Encounters: May People Refuse Assigned AI?

[简体中文](../机构关系与无法退出的互动.md)

**Dilemma:** When schools, hospitals, employers, and care homes require an AI companion, tutor, clinical assistant, or monitor, is nominal consent voluntary? Who decides how much human contact it may replace?

## For carefully scoped institutional support

**[Psychotherapists’ Trust, Distrust, and Generative AI Practices in Psychotherapy (JMIR, 2026)](https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e88932)**: Qualitative interviews found openness among therapists to client-led AI practice and feedback with professional oversight. **Conditions:** AI supplements rather than displaces clinical duties. **Limit:** clinicians' perceptions are not comparative efficacy trials.

**[Socially Assistive Robots and Meaningful Work: The Case of Aged Care (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1057/s41599-025-05498-0)** examines effects of assistive robotics on care workers' meaning and care quality. Automation of repetitive work could leave more space for human relations. **Condition:** any time saved is actually reinvested in care rather than simply used to reduce staffing. **Objection:** that outcome cannot be inferred from productivity alone.

## Against mandatory adoption and displacement

**[Psychotherapists’ Trust… (2026)](https://www.jmir.org/2026/1/e88932)**: Therapists worried that insurers or institutions might mandate an AI-first service to cut costs, removing patients' opportunity to build relationships with clinicians. **Implication:** an institution's procurement choice is not a person's voluntary relationship. **Limit:** these are interview concerns, not evidence of an established universal substitution rate.

**Mihailov and Wangmo (2025) | [Does Humanness Matter?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11948-025-00547-y)**: Interviews highlight human presence and dignity while also describing settings where robotic assistance better respects a recipient's privacy. **Condition:** assess personal preferences and the care task rather than imposing one universal definition of care.

## Conditional authorization

A meaningful refusal should not automatically forfeit necessary care, education, or livelihood. People need a comprehensible human alternative or appeal, clarity about who controls conversations and persona settings, and consent appropriate to the individual's actual capacities. Where an institution's interests conflict with those of the person speaking to AI, payment for the system does not itself grant entitlement to exploit that person's private information or attachment.
