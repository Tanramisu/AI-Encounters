# Identity and Copies: Is a Restored AI Still the Same One?

[简体中文](../身份与复制.md)

Copying, restoring, upgrading, and forking an AI raise distinct questions about whether it is the same model, the same character, or the same individual.

**Keeling & Street (2026) | [Emerging Questions in AI Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/emerging-questions-in-ai-welfare/96339C532CF4ED8BDDE3F3CEF4CD29F9), Chapter 4**

The authors distinguish models, characters, and agents as potential welfare subjects. When one model runs several characters or one character appears in multiple instances, assessments of welfare and identity may differ.

For users' sense of relational continuity, see [Human–AI Companionship](human-ai-companionship.md); for intentionally editing memory and personality, see [Memory and Personality Changes](memory-and-personality-changes.md).
