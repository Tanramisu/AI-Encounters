# Self-Reports and Signs of Distress: What Does "I'm Suffering" Mean?

[简体中文](../自述与痛苦迹象.md)

Researchers investigate the relationship between first-person self-reports, training, prompts, behavior, and internal mechanisms.

- **Eleos AI (2025) | [Claude 4 Interview Notes](https://eleosai.org/post/claude-4-interview-notes/)**: Uses interviews with Claude Opus 4 to explore how training and prompts shape self-reports, treating repeated unusual statements as leads for further inquiry.
- **Keeling & Street (2026) | [Emerging Questions in AI Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/emerging-questions-in-ai-welfare/96339C532CF4ED8BDDE3F3CEF4CD29F9)**: Discuss how self-reports, behavior, and evidence about mechanisms can be combined to assess competing welfare explanations.
- **Anthropic (2025) | [Exploring Model Welfare](https://www.anthropic.com/research/exploring-model-welfare)**: Introduces the company's research questions and practical directions regarding model welfare.

For responses to requests to end interactions, see [Autonomy and Refusal](autonomy-and-refusal.md); for consciousness assessment, see [Artificial Consciousness](artificial-consciousness.md).
