# Public Deliberation, Political Persuasion, and Disclosure: May AI Pose as Citizens?

[简体中文](../公共讨论政治劝服与知情.md)

**Dilemma:** AI may help people understand public issues and reduce hostility, or covertly impersonate citizens and influence political views. The ethical distinction concerns identity, purpose, and informed participation, not merely persuasive effectiveness.

## For understanding across disagreement

**[Reducing Political Polarization Through Conversations with Artificial Intelligence (2026)](https://academic.oup.com/jcmc/article/31/2/zmag003/8551324)**: Two preregistered online experiments (N=1,035) found that receptive, attentive AI dialogue could reduce some negative feelings toward political outgroups and increase willingness to converse. **For:** AI can assist understanding of opposing views. **Limit:** short-term attitudes are not long-term democratic outcomes.

**[Leveraging AI for Democratic Discourse (PNAS, 2023)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2311627120)**: In a randomized study, linguistic assistance to one participant improved the other participant's reported conversation quality. **Condition:** AI improves communication between real people rather than secretly directing them.

## Against covert political influence and fake citizens

**[Persuading Voters Using Human–AI Dialogues (Nature, 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09771-9)**: Randomized studies in the settings of three real elections found that political AI conversations could shift some participants' candidate preferences. **Implication:** AI is a meaningful political influence channel, making undisclosed objectives especially relevant. **Limit:** experimental influence is not proof of altering real election outcomes at scale.

**[LLM-Generated Messages Can Persuade Humans on Policy Issues (Nature Communications, 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-61345-5)**: Three preregistered experiments (N=4,829) found shifts in policy attitudes from AI-written messages compared with neutral controls, broadly comparable to lay human writing. **Implication:** both human and AI political speech can influence views.

**[Testing Theories of Political Persuasion Using AI (PNAS, 2025)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2412815122)**: Some more elaborate customization and interaction conditions did not significantly outperform generic messaging. Avoid asserting that every use of personalization necessarily increases persuasion.

## Conditional democratic boundaries

Distinguish **open debate, assisting real citizens, authorized representation, and persuasion concealed behind fake identities or sponsors**. AI involvement is not inherently illegitimate, but hidden control and impersonation can deprive others of information relevant to fair public deliberation. Claims should remain contestable, with real sponsors and controllers identifiable.
