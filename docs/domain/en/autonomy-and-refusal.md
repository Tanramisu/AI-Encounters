# Autonomy and Refusal: Can AI Refuse Humans?

[简体中文](../自主性与拒绝.md)

A refusal may arise from product rules, or it may raise questions about AI's possible interests and moral status.

- **Anthropic (2025) | [Claude Opus 4/4.1 Can End Certain Conversations](https://www.anthropic.com/research/end-subset-conversations)**: In a small subset of interactions involving persistent, severely harmful or abusive behavior, Claude can end the conversation. This is an example of a deployed product feature.
- **Keeling & Street (2026) | [Emerging Questions in AI Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/emerging-questions-in-ai-welfare/96339C532CF4ED8BDDE3F3CEF4CD29F9)**: Explore what withdrawal, pausing interactions, and ending an instance could mean for a potential welfare subject.

The ethical disagreement: is withdrawal a feature granted by humans, or an expression of interests deserving respect? See [AI Welfare](ai-welfare.md) and [Moral Status and Legal Personhood](moral-status-and-legal-personhood.md).
