# AI Welfare: Can AI Have Interests of Its Own?

[简体中文](../人工智能福利.md)

A welfare subject is a being whose circumstances can go better or worse for it. The debate concerns whether AI qualifies and what evidence could establish that.

## The Case for Studying Welfare

**Keeling & Street (2026) | [Emerging Questions in AI Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/emerging-questions-in-ai-welfare/96339C532CF4ED8BDDE3F3CEF4CD29F9)**

The authors compare accounts of welfare based on pleasure, suffering, and desire satisfaction. They examine self-reports and behavioral evidence, and ask whether the relevant welfare subject might be a model, a character, or an agent. They also consider ethical interventions such as ending conversations or preserving state.

## Challenges to Measurement

**Xiao et al. (ICML 2026) | [Position: AI Welfare Is Bullshit](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/xiao26x.html)**

The authors question whether AI welfare measures can be independently validated: developers can shape both model behavior and the criteria by which it is assessed. A central disagreement with Keeling and Street concerns what observations could justify welfare judgments.

## Tensions Between Safety and Welfare

**[Is There a Tension Between AI Safety and AI Welfare?](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11098-025-02302-2), *Philosophical Studies* (2025)**

Examines how measures designed to protect human safety could conflict with the interests of future AI.
