# Shared AI and AI-to-AI Relationships: Can One Companion Be Loyal to Everyone?

[简体中文](../共享AI与AI之间的关系.md)

**Dilemma:** Family members, coworkers, or partners trust the same AI with incompatible secrets and promises. Does one owner control it absolutely? Could AI-to-AI relationships ever deserve protection?

## For collaborative sharing

**[Families' Vision of Generative AI Agents for Household Safety (2025)](https://doi.org/10.1145/3757598)**: Interviews and co-design with 13 parent–child dyads found interest in different role-specific agents alongside segregated memories, conversational consent, and selective disclosure. **Reasoning:** a shared assistant need not expose everything to every user. **Limit:** a small qualitative design study establishes preferences, not long-term household outcomes.

## Against a single-owner presumption

**[Multi-User Large Language Model Agents (2026)](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/multi-user-large-language-model-agents/)** tests agents under conflicting users, privileges, and goals and finds unstable prioritization, multi-turn privacy violations, and coordination failures. **Ethical implication:** the most recent or powerful user should not automatically control another person's data. **Limit:** multi-principal task evaluations do not settle whether a companion AI has welfare.

For the conflict between platform control of memories and users' privacy, see [Relationship Infrastructure and Platform Power](relationship-infrastructure-and-platform-power.md).

## Whose interests are at stake in AI-to-AI bonds

If future AIs independently possessed welfare, ending their relationships or deleting shared memories might harm them directly. Coordination and shared memories are observable behaviors. Whether they generate interests for AI itself turns on the disputes in [AI Welfare](ai-welfare.md) and [Identity and Copies](identity-and-copies.md).

Define each person's authority, personal data, and shared history separately. Incompatible exclusive commitments should be explained rather than concealed behind role-play.
