# Artificial Consciousness: Could AI Have Subjective Experience?

[简体中文](../人工意识.md)

Consciousness research examines the relationship between observable behavior, computational mechanisms, and subjective experience.

- **Butlin et al. (2023) | [Consciousness in Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.08708)**: Derive indicators for computational systems from theories such as global workspace theory and higher-order theories, then apply them to AI systems of the time. How these indicators are interpreted depends on the underlying theory of consciousness.
- **Keeling & Street (2026) | [Emerging Questions in AI Welfare](https://www.cambridge.org/core/elements/emerging-questions-in-ai-welfare/96339C532CF4ED8BDDE3F3CEF4CD29F9), Chapter 3**: Examine how behavior, self-reports, and internal mechanisms can distinguish between competing explanations of consciousness or welfare.

For concrete cases, see [Self-Reports and Signs of Distress](self-reports-and-distress.md); for possible interests of AI itself, see [AI Welfare](ai-welfare.md).
